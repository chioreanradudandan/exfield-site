Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026 · Applies to the Exfield mobile app for iOS and Android.

The short version

Exfield keeps your habit data on your device. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, no ads, and no analytics. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

What the app stores

Your habits — names, colors, settings, reminder times, and the days you mark — are saved in a file on your device only. Deleting the app deletes this data. We have no copy and no way to access it.

Notifications

If you set a daily reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device. Reminder times never leave your phone.

Purchases

The one-time Pro unlock is processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play — we never see your payment details. To validate the purchase and enable restoring it on a reinstall, the app uses RevenueCat, which processes an anonymous app-generated identifier and your purchase receipt. No name, email, or other personal information is involved.

Children

Exfield does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.
